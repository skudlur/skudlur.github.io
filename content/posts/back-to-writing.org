It has been more than two years since I last wrote anything here. The last
post went up in June 2024. A lot has happened since (the [[/about/][About]] page has
the summary), and somewhere in there I stopped writing in public. I want to fix
that.

So here is the plan: *one small post every weekday*. Small means a few hundred
words and one idea, a paper or a bit of news. Sometimes it will be just one plot
or one surprising number. I started out writing long blogs but I think that led
me to burn out. This time around, the objective is to write smaller, yet
meaningful thoughts, measurements, reports on some of my open-source work and
things that interest me.

What I'll write about:

- *Memory.* Caches, TLBs, DRAM and the odd things it does, measured on hardware
  I own.
- *CPUs.* Mostly build logs from [[https://github.com/skudlur/diablo][diablo]], my out-of-order RISC-V core, which
  I am rewriting in Bluespec.
- *Interconnects.* Be it CXL or chip interconnects like TileLink and AXI. I've
  been poking at CXL with [[https://github.com/skudlur/TraceCXL][TraceCXL]].
- *Things I build.* A few repositories that have been sitting private will go
  public over the next few weeks, and each one deserves a write-up.

A few rules for myself:

1. Every number comes with the machine, compiler and command that produced it,
   so you can rerun it.
2. If I'm unsure about something, I'll say so.
3. I will try and keep the blogs as concise as I can (with the occasional
   rambling or longer reporting on something truly intriguing).

Next up: the memory wall, the elephant in the room.
